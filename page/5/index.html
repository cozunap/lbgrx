LB Graph-X & Printing Inc. firmly believe that every client's project holds a solution. Therefore, we are dedicated to delivering exemplary services tailored to their specific needs and within the agreed time frame

LB Graph-X & Printing Inc. commenced its journey 20 years ago in the vibrant city of Washington High, NY. Since opening our doors, we have continually evolved to meet the dynamic demands of our valued customers and the ever-changing marketplace. While initially recognized as a leading graphic and design agency, our growth has expanded beyond those horizons – today, we proudly offer cutting-edge printing solutions as well. At LB Graph-X & Printing Inc., we are your dedicated experts in graphic communications, empowering you to broadcast your message without limits.

LB Graph-X & Printing Inc. stands as your comprehensive destination for top-notch graphic design, printing services, and more. Throughout our journey, we’ve introduced a myriad of new services, embracing modern innovations in large format printing for applications like vehicle wraps, banners, murals, and beyond. Additionally, we’ve made a significant impact in the printing industry for custom t-shirts and garnered recognition for our expertise in food photography, particularly for restaurant menus. Our commitment to excellence and versatility positions us as your go-to partner for a wide array of creative and printing needs.

Offset Printing

LB Graph-X specializes in offset printing of brochures, post cards, newsletters, posters, presentation folders, magazines, stationery, business cards, folders, and forms.

Digital Printing

Make your company shine with premium digital printing. We offer a wide range of printed materials, including banners, posters, adhesive signs, banner stands, yard signs, and more.

Graphic Design

With more than 20 years of experience in graphic design, LB Graph-X delivers top-notch quality. Our team collaborates to transform your brand into a standout identity.
